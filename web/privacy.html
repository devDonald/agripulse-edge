AgriPulse Edge

Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

AgriPulse helps farmers and field teams turn crop images into practical guidance. We collect only what is needed to provide, secure and improve that service.

Information we collect

We may receive crop images, diagnosis results, language preference, device and browser details, approximate location supplied by a field team, and contact details used to provide support. WhatsApp messages are received through Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform.

How we use information

  • To analyse crop images and return diagnosis and treatment guidance.
  • To sync field records across authorised web, mobile and WhatsApp experiences.
  • To send requested service messages, voice guidance and operational alerts.
  • To protect the service, prevent abuse and improve reliability.

Images and AI processing

Crop images may be processed by configured Meta AI model providers to generate a requested diagnosis. Do not upload faces, identity documents or unrelated personal information. A crop diagnosis is decision support, not a substitute for a qualified agronomist.

Sharing and retention

We use infrastructure and model providers needed to operate AgriPulse. We do not sell personal information. Records are retained only for operational, safety and research purposes, or until an authorised user requests deletion, subject to legal and security requirements.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion by emailing privacy@zentreeca.org or using our data deletion page.
