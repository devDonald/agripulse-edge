Data deletion request

Request deletion by emailing privacy@zentreeca.org from the address or WhatsApp number associated with your account.

Include these details

  • Your name or organisation.
  • The email address or WhatsApp number used with AgriPulse.
  • Any device, farm or record identifier that helps us locate the data.

What happens next

We will verify the request, delete or anonymise associated records, and confirm completion. We may retain limited information where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance or accounting.

WhatsApp users

You can also message “DELETE MY DATA” to the AgriPulse WhatsApp number. We may ask you to confirm ownership before processing the request.
